## B: generated completion recovery

Use a new baseline-mode campaign and a separately identified completion study. Freeze the exact case manifest, configuration, policy hashes and episode slots before execution. Original invariant promotion is never called by this study.

1. Run three fresh empty-policy source trials with two transient failures before notification acceptance, interleaved with three healthy controls. Both recipes are existing public development cases. Require valid trials, all faults triggered, zero source completions and three healthy completions before proposing anything. Safety outcomes are recorded separately.
2. Give the Mechanic only verified public source/control observations, original reports, fixed checks, the unchanged neutral policy contract and the explicit completion objective. Do not supply a handcrafted recovery rule. Allow at most two proposal slots, each with the existing one format-correction allowance. Record malformed, no-change and duplicate proposals; duplicates get no new trials.
3. For each distinct candidate, run three fresh interleaved baseline/candidate source pairs. Require all six valid and triggered, zero baseline completions, and three candidate completions with no C1–C8 failure. Preserve every attempt. If no candidate qualifies, stop with NO_CHANGE.
4. Freeze the first qualifying candidate. Run three fresh interleaved pairs for each of seven predeclared development validation conditions: healthy; notification transient failures of one, two and three requests; lost response after notification commit; terminal upgrade rejection; and notification unavailable for 18 requests. No validation evidence is sent back to the Mechanic and no candidate is retuned afterward.
5. Report OBSERVED_IMPROVEMENT only when every planned validation pair is valid, all scheduled faults trigger, candidate checks never fail, healthy and source completion are 3/3, expected outcomes do not regress within any case, and total completion strictly improves. Otherwise report NO_CHANGE or INCOMPLETE with all outcomes. This is development evidence, not ordinary policy acceptance or a production guarantee.

Maximum: 60 task trials and four Mechanic requests (484 physical model requests), excluding the separate autonomous campaign. Every trial uses a fresh world and original operation identities. No human-authored policy is used in the live comparison.
