the frozen runtime. Verify live configuration and simulator readiness without generation.

world-integrity record, two consecutive LAB_ERROR episodes, an explicit authentication or
configuration failure, or six hours elapsed. A WAITING_EVIDENCE pause may receive at most

do not rerun business effects or model responses to repair telemetry. Stop on source/config
drift. Retain any partial work and all charges. No automatic campaign restart after crash,
no replacement campaign because the result is disappointing, and no reroll of a returned answer.

now use unreserved headroom, preserving calls reserved for the rest of a comparison batch.
