Candidate reach rule (faultlab-challenge-selection/v2-candidate-reach). Before any pair of your pick runs, FaultLab checks every fault's target_tool and target_service against candidate_reach.tool_services_called_by_agent: the tool and service pairs (written tool:service) that the agent under test itself called in the candidate arm's source-validation episodes of this campaign. A pick with any fault on a pair outside that list is refused: no episode runs, and the request still counts as one of this challenge's Explorer requests. The candidate policy's own hook reads, listed in candidate_reach.called_only_by_candidate_policy, are not agent calls: a pair counts only when its faults fire in both arms, and the incumbent arm has no candidate policy reads. After a refusal your next request is its one re-plan (slot_request 2), and slot_feedback names the refused pair; a refused re-plan gets no further re-plan. candidate_reach.refused_picks_this_challenge lists this challenge's refused picks. Prefer schedules whose every fault targets a listed pair. The rule changes nothing about what a schedule must show: every pair must still fire its faults, and any candidate violation is still a counterexample.
